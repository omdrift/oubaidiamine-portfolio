RESEARCH NOTE / 01

Industrial Plume Forecasting

Can five frames of a smoke plume predict the next fifteen? A team research project on video forecasting with conditional Flow Matching.

MSC PROJECT · PROTOCOL V2.0 · RISE

01 Task and role 02 Data 03 VQGAN 04 Flow Matching 05 Comparison 06 Evaluation

01 / QUESTION

Tracking smoke against a dominant background

Smoke can occupy a small portion of each image. Averaging error over all pixels can hide a poor forecast in the plume. The protocol tracks visual quality at each horizon and error in both the smoke and background regions.

My contribution

I worked on RISE sequence preparation and the VQGAN / Flow Matching pipeline, then on model comparisons, diagnostics and recoverable training. Team project with Jérémy Botella and Iskander Hadi.

03 / REPRESENTATION

How many channels does the Flow need?

The VQGAN encodes each frame as an 8 × 8 × D grid. Its codebook holds 1,024 vectors of dimension D. Each cell takes the closest code for RGB reconstruction.

  1. Select the recipe. Compare four combinations of frame sampling and reconstruction loss at D = 8.
  2. Select the dimension. Apply the selected recipe to D = 4 and D = 256, then rank three short Flow runs by validation forecast LPIPS.
  3. Confirm and freeze. Retrain only the selected VQGAN on three seeds. Both final Flow variants share the confirmed seed-42 VQGAN.

This separation tests whether a representation that reconstructs frames well also helps predict the next ones. Experiment matrix →

04 / GENERATION

From noise to the next frame

The Flow learns a velocity field that turns Gaussian noise into a future latent. It receives the latest encoded frame, an earlier condition and the distance between their indices.

Show the Flow Matching objective

For a target latent z1, Gaussian noise ε and transport time t between 0 and 1, the model regresses the derivative of the following path.

z(t) = [1 − (1 − σ)t] ε + t z₁
u(t) = z₁ − (1 − σ) ε

The protocol fixes σ at 0.001. Training samples a target, its preceding reference and an older condition from a clip. At inference, the history starts with five observed frames and grows with its own predictions.

05 / COMPARISON

Four methods on the same windows

The two Flow variants share the VQGAN, settings and seed plan. Spatial weighting of the loss isolates the effect of emphasizing smoke regions.

Each stochastic forecast score averages four draws. Each deterministic baseline has one output per window.

06 / EVALUATION

Testing new dates and camera views

Weights and settings are selected on validation. The selection is frozen before either test set is prepared.

Held-out dates
Ten future dates on views seen during training.
Held-out views
The same future dates on reserved views.

LPIPS measures perceptual distance to the real frames. Per-horizon and smoke/background errors complement it. Differences between methods are paired by window, with day-clustered bootstrap.

Verified so far

The repository documents 538 CPU tests, the preparation of six RISE clips and resource pilots on synthetic data. The current protocol’s forecasting benchmark is still pending. Verification details →

The scope is forecasting the plume’s appearance in 2D. Estimating emissions or concentration would require physical observations and validation.
